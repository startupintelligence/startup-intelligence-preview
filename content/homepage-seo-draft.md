## Draft body copy

### What does Startup Intelligence do?

Startup Intelligence helps startups, small businesses, and entrepreneurs establish and improve their digital presence. We bring website development, branding, digital marketing, SEO, and AI optimisation together to help businesses communicate clearly, reach relevant audiences, and build a stronger foundation for growth.

Every business has different goals, challenges, and customers. Our approach begins with understanding those needs before deciding which digital solutions are appropriate.

### What digital services can help your business grow?

**Web development:** Build a professional website that communicates your offering, supports visitors, and makes it easier for potential customers to take action.

**Branding:** Create a consistent visual identity and brand presentation that helps your business communicate its personality and values.

**Digital marketing:** Plan digital campaigns and communication around your business goals and target audience.

**SEO services:** Improve website structure, content relevance, technical accessibility, and discoverability through search engines.

**AI optimisation:** Structure useful, clear, and credible website content so AI-powered search and answer systems can better interpret it.

These services can work independently or together, depending on your priorities and budget.

### How do we turn a startup into a brand?

Our approach connects four stages:

1. **Strategy:** Understand your business, audience, positioning, and objectives.
2. **Design:** Develop a coherent visual identity and user experience.
3. **Technology:** Build and refine the digital systems needed to support your business.
4. **Growth:** Improve discoverability, content, marketing, and user journeys over time.

The process should be tailored to each project rather than treated as a fixed solution for every business.

### Why does your business need a strong digital presence?

Customers often research a business online before contacting it. An unclear website, inconsistent branding, or difficult user experience can make it harder for potential customers to understand your offering.

A useful digital presence should explain what you do, who you help, why your services matter, and how people can take the next step. It should also work well on mobile devices, load efficiently, and make important information easy to find.

### What can you expect from working with us?

We aim to build solutions around defined business needs, clear deliverables, and practical priorities. The scope, schedule, pricing, and success measures should be discussed before a project begins.

We will not promise specific search rankings or guaranteed business growth. Instead, the focus is on building a sound digital foundation and identifying meaningful opportunities for improvement.

### Which service is right for your business?

The right starting point depends on your goals. A new business may need a clear website and brand identity first. A business with an established website may benefit from a technical SEO review, better content, or a more considered marketing plan. A discussion of your current situation can help identify appropriate next steps.

### Ready to build your brand?

Whether you are launching a new business or improving an existing online presence, the first step is understanding where you are now and what you want to achieve.

Request a growth audit to discuss your current digital presence, priorities, and possible next steps.
