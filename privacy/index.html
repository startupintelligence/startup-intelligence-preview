Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

This policy describes the Startup Intelligence preview website and its current discovery brief. The preview is not a confirmed booking system. Update this policy before connecting any new collection, analytics, advertising, payment or account service.

Information you provide

The homepage discovery brief runs in your browser. When you submit it, the page prepares an email draft addressed to startupintelligence1@gmail.com using your device’s email application. The website does not transmit the brief to a server or store it. Nothing is emailed unless you review the draft and choose to send it. If you send it, your email provider and the recipient’s mailbox will process and retain it under their own settings and policies. The form is not a calendar booking: a call time must be arranged separately. Do not include passwords, payment details or sensitive customer information.

Technical information and hosting

The preview website is hosted using GitHub Pages. Hosting providers may process technical information such as request logs, IP addresses, browser details and timestamps to deliver and secure the service. Consult GitHub Pages documentation and GitHub’s Privacy Statement for their practices.

Cookies, analytics and tracking

The site uses browser local storage to remember your display-theme choice (`si-theme`). This preference remains in your browser until you clear site data and is not sent to Startup Intelligence by the site. The inspected site code does not include third-party analytics scripts, session replay or advertising pixels. Review this statement if scripts or external services are added. Your browser or hosting platform may still use technical mechanisms necessary to deliver the site.

External links

This website may link to third-party websites. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

Children

This website is general informational content and is not designed specifically to solicit personal information from children.

Changes and contact

This policy must be reviewed before a server-side form, booking calendar, analytics, advertising, payments or account system is connected. To report a concern, use the Contact page. This page is a plain-language summary, not legal advice or a guarantee of compliance with every applicable law.
