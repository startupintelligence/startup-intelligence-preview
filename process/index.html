THE DELIVERY MODEL

A clear sequence. Shared decisions.

You should know what is happening, what is needed from you and what must be approved at each stage.

01

Discovery

We learn about your business, audience, desired outcome, existing assets and constraints.

02

Scope & proposal

You receive the proposed deliverables, exclusions, price, milestones, responsibilities and any third-party costs.

03

Design & implementation

We work through the agreed direction and build, sharing progress at defined review points.

04

Review & validation

We check the agreed requirements, responsive layouts, links and key interactions that are in scope.

05

Handover & next steps

We explain the delivered work, access and any maintenance or ongoing work that has been agreed.

SHARED RESPONSIBILITY

What we need from you.

Good delivery depends on timely decisions and access to the right materials.

Helpful inputs

  • Business details and target audience
  • Existing logo, brand and content assets
  • Access to relevant platforms where needed
  • One decision-maker for feedback

What we document

  • Deliverables and exclusions
  • Timeline and review milestones
  • Payment and third-party costs
  • Handover and agreed support
