GOOD QUESTIONS, CLEAR ANSWERS

FAQs before we get started.

Here are the practical details most people want to know before starting a project.

What services do you offer?

We focus on custom website development and SEO & growth optimization. Depending on your needs, a project can also include strategy, UX/UI design, performance, accessibility, technical SEO and suitable integrations.

How much does a website cost?

Cost depends on page count, design requirements, content, integrations and complexity. The budget selector is only a conversation starter—not a fixed price list. We confirm deliverables, exclusions, price and payment terms in a written proposal before paid work begins.

How long will my project take?

Timing depends on scope, content readiness, integrations and review time. After discovery, we can propose milestones and a realistic timeline for the agreed scope rather than promise an unsupported deadline.

Will my website work on mobile?

Responsive behavior is part of our intended delivery process. The agreed project should be checked at mobile, tablet and desktop sizes before launch.

Can you add a backend, database or AI features?

Potentially, after we review the requirements, security, data handling, hosting and ongoing costs. These features need a suitable technical plan; they are not automatically included in every website project.

Do you guarantee Google rankings or a specific Lighthouse score?

No. SEO results and performance scores depend on many factors. We can implement and measure sound technical foundations, explain the findings and prioritize improvements, but we do not guarantee rankings or a perfect score.

How do I start, and is the discovery call booked instantly?

Use the discovery brief to prepare an enquiry. At present, the website opens an email draft for you to review and send; it does not send your details automatically or reserve a calendar slot. We arrange a time separately.

What happens after I contact you?

We clarify your goals, audience, scope, constraints and budget, then agree on the next step. Any paid work should begin only after the scope and commercial terms are confirmed in writing.

What is AEO and how is it different from SEO?

Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) makes useful information easier for answer-oriented search experiences to understand. It builds on good SEO with direct answers, descriptive headings, accurate facts, clear entities, useful FAQs and structured data that matches visible content.

What is GEO for a website?

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) helps make a website's information clear, trustworthy and easy for generative search and AI assistants to interpret. Useful foundations include original expertise, consistent business details, crawlable pages, descriptive internal links and well-structured answers. No one can guarantee inclusion in an AI answer.

What does AIO mean in digital marketing?

AI Optimization (AIO) is used broadly for preparing content and digital experiences for AI-assisted discovery and workflows. We treat it as a practical extension of technical SEO, AEO and GEO—not as a guaranteed ranking product.

Can you build a backend, dashboard or web application?

These projects can be assessed separately. We first define users, data, permissions, workflows, hosting, security, backups, integrations and ongoing operating costs. Backend functionality is not included automatically in a basic marketing website.

Can you add AI features or automation?

Potentially, when there is a clear user problem and a responsible implementation path. We assess data, API costs, privacy, accuracy, human review, failure handling and maintenance before proposing an AI feature.

Do you guarantee Google rankings, AI citations or a perfect Lighthouse score?

No. Search rankings, AI citations and performance scores depend on factors outside a developer's control. We can implement sound foundations, test agreed pages, explain evidence and prioritize improvements, but we do not guarantee a position, citation or score.

Who owns the website and its content after launch?

Ownership, source files, licences, third-party subscriptions, domain access and handover arrangements should be explicitly stated in the written proposal.

What is SXO and why does it matter?

Search Experience Optimization (SXO) connects search visibility with the experience people have after they click. It considers page relevance, clear navigation, mobile usability, loading speed, accessibility, helpful content and a useful next action. The goal is not only to attract a visit, but to help the visitor complete the task they came to do.
