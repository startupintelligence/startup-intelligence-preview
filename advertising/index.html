FEES & MEDIA SPEND

Know what you pay, and where it goes.

Advertising budgets are paid to the relevant platform. Our management fee is separate and is confirmed in the written proposal.

Two separate costs:
1. Campaign management fee paid to Startup Intelligence, as agreed.
2. Media spend paid to Meta or Google through the client’s advertising account.

Results depend on the offer, market, creative, audience, landing page, budget and platform conditions. We do not guarantee leads, sales or return on ad spend.
