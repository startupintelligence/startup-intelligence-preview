01 / BUSINESS

Business websites

Company information, service descriptions, trust-building content and clear inquiry paths.

  • Home and service pages
  • About and contact information
  • Responsive layouts
02 / STARTUP

Startup and SaaS

Product positioning, feature explanations, pricing information and launch messaging.

  • Value proposition and use cases
  • Product and feature pages
  • Calls to action
03 / COMMERCE

E-commerce

Product discovery and purchase journeys, subject to platform, payment and operations requirements.

  • Product and category structure
  • Cart and checkout planning
  • Payment-provider integration review
04 / PORTFOLIO

Creator and portfolio sites

Personal profiles, services, selected work and contact information using real, permissioned content.

  • Profile and service pages
  • Project or work presentation
  • Contact path
05 / DATA

Dashboards and web apps

Data-rich interfaces and workflows that need a separate technical and security review.

  • Information architecture
  • Role and account requirements
  • Backend and deployment planning
06 / CONTENT

Education and publishing

Structured content for lessons, articles, resources and knowledge collections.

  • Content organization
  • Search and navigation planning
  • Accessibility considerations
