SERVICE QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Understand what we do and how different services are scoped.

What is your main service?

Website design and development are our primary focus. We build business websites and can assess more advanced web requirements separately.

Do you also provide SEO?

Yes. SEO and digital growth support can include technical foundations, page structure, metadata and practical content recommendations. The exact scope is agreed for each project.

Do you manage Meta Ads and Google Ads?

Yes. Paid advertising setup and management are available as additional services. The proposal will define campaign scope, creative, optimization, tracking and reporting.

Is ad spend included in your fee?

No. Platform media spend is paid directly by the client to Meta or Google and is separate from our service fee.

Can I choose only one service?

Yes. You can enquire about website development, SEO support or paid advertising independently. We will confirm whether the requested scope is a suitable fit.
