CLEAR ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions

Practical information about scope, process, pricing and what to expect.

Does the Contact form send my information automatically?

No. The current form prepares an email draft in your email application. You review the recipient and content, then choose whether to send it. The website does not currently store the enquiry in a database.

What should I include in my project brief?

Describe the outcome you want, who the website is for, the pages or features you expect, any existing website, your approximate budget and your preferred timeline. A short, clear brief is enough to begin.

Why do you ask for a budget?

A budget range helps us suggest a realistic scope and avoid proposing features that do not fit your constraints. You can choose that you are unsure or want to discuss a custom budget.

Can I contact you without completing the form?

Yes. You can email startupintelligence1@gmail.com directly. Include a short project summary and avoid sending passwords, payment information or sensitive personal data.

Does submitting the form book a discovery call?

No. The form does not connect to a calendar or reserve a time. It prepares an email draft; any meeting time is arranged separately after contact.

How will my enquiry details be used?

The current browser form does not submit or store your details on the website. If you choose to send the email, the information is handled by your email provider and the recipient. Review the Privacy Policy for current website practices.
