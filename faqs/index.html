COMMON QUESTIONS

Straight answers before you start.

If your question is not covered here, send it with your project brief and we can clarify it before you commit.

What services do you provide?

Our primary service is website design and development. We also provide SEO and digital growth support, and scoped Meta Ads and Google Ads campaign management.

What is the minimum website project budget?

Website projects start from ₹5,000. The final quote depends on pages, content, design complexity, functionality, integrations and timeline.

Is the advertising budget included in your fee?

No. The campaign management fee and the media spend paid to Meta or Google are separate costs. Both are explained before work begins.

Do you guarantee Google rankings or advertising results?

No. Search rankings, leads, sales and advertising returns depend on many factors outside any provider’s control. We agree the work and reporting, not guaranteed outcomes.

How long does a website take?

Timing depends on the agreed scope, content readiness, functionality, feedback and integrations. We provide a project timeline with the proposal.

Can you work with my existing website?

Often, yes. We first review the current platform, access, technical condition and what needs to change. Some legacy systems may require a different approach.

Do you provide custom functionality?

Custom forms, integrations and workflows can be assessed during discovery. Feasibility, security, maintenance and third-party costs are considered before quoting.

How do I start?

Send a project enquiry with the goal, current website if any, indicative budget and timeline. The enquiry is not a booking or contract; the next step is confirmed separately.

Are you a registered company?

Startup Intelligence is presented as an independent digital services initiative. It is not represented here as a registered company.

Who owns the website after handover?

Ownership, source files, hosting access, third-party licences and any ongoing maintenance responsibilities are specified in the written proposal and handover terms.
